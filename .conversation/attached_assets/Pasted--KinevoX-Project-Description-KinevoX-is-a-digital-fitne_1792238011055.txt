# KinevoX — Project Description

**KinevoX** is a digital fitness and wellness platform designed to help beginners build healthier lifestyles through personalized workout plans, exercise guidance, healthy habits, and progress tracking.

The platform will provide a simple, accessible experience that allows users to define their goals, identify their fitness level, select available equipment, and receive suitable workout recommendations.

## Main Objectives
- Create personalized workout plans based on user preferences and fitness levels.
- Provide an exercise library with instructions and relevant media.
- Encourage healthy habits related to sleep, hydration, balanced nutrition, and recovery.
- Track workout completion and user progress.
- Offer an AI-powered assistant for general fitness and wellness guidance, with appropriate safety limitations.
- Support Arabic and English from the initial development stage.

## Target Audience
Beginners who want to improve their fitness, develop healthy habits, and monitor their progress through an easy-to-use digital platform.

## Technology
- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Vite for the development environment
- Git and GitHub for version control
- Supabase for authentication and database services in a later phase

## Initial Scope
The first version will focus on the homepage, bilingual interface, user onboarding questionnaire, reviewed exercise library, basic workout recommendations, and progress tracking.

Advanced AI features, payments, mobile applications, and camera-based analysis will be considered in future versions.

## Safety and Privacy
KinevoX will provide general fitness and wellness information, not medical diagnosis or treatment. User data will be handled with appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

## Long-Term Vision
Build KinevoX into a scalable fitness and wellness company that combines technology, personalization, and practical guidance to help people make sustainable progress.
